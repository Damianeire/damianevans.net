I'm a double bassist from Perth, Western Australia. I came to Ireland in 1997 and have worked here as a jazz and traditional musician ever since.

In jazz I've led my own trio, co-led the Boylan/Buckley/Evans Trio, and played with visiting artists including Terell Stafford, Jonathan Kreisberg, Tim Warfield and Soweto Kinch, and with fellow Australians Jamie Oehlers and Paul Williamson. In traditional music I've toured with Alan Kelly, Arty McGlynn and Seán Keane, and recorded with Sharon Shannon and Gerry O'Connor, among others. I've also toured India with Honor Heffernan, the Dominican Republic with Emilie Conway, and Ireland and Portugal with Chad Dughi.

I set up the Galway Jazz Club and the Galway Jazz Festival before moving to Dublin. There I completed a PhD in jazz studies in 2016 at the Conservatory of Music and Drama (then DIT, now TU Dublin), supported by a Fiosraigh Scholarship and the Dean of Graduate Students Award. My thesis looked at the Dublin jazz scene, past and present. I went on to lecture at University College Dublin, co-edit *The Musicology Review*, found the Documenting Jazz conference, run a jazz studies reading group, serve as Honorary Secretary of the Society for Musicology in Ireland (2019 to 2021), and act as book review editor for *Jazz Perspectives*.

I've also taught music in primary schools and early childhood settings with Music Generation Meath and Louth. I now coordinate an alternative education centre.

Outside music I play a lot of chess, and I build small tools and apps, mostly for musicians. Some of them are on the [Projects](/projects/) page.
