We met once a month at DCU's St Patrick's Campus to talk through papers, articles and book chapters that shaped jazz studies, with a particular interest in race, gender and identity. People came from all disciplines and at all levels. The group no longer meets, but the reading list may still be useful.

## What we read

**February 2019: Music cognition and improvisation.** Vijay Iyer, "Improvisation, Action Understanding, and Music Cognition with and without Bodies", in *The Oxford Handbook of Critical Improvisation Studies*, vol. 1 (2014), 74–90. [doi:10.1093/oxfordhb/9780195370935.013.014](https://doi.org/10.1093/oxfordhb/9780195370935.013.014)

**January 2019: Authorship and works.** Gabriel Solis, "'A Unique Chunk of Jazz Reality': Authorship, Musical Work Concepts, and Thelonious Monk's Live Recordings from the Five Spot, 1958", *Ethnomusicology* 48, no. 3 (2004): 315–347.

**November 2018: Authorship, authority and authenticity.** Tony Whyton, "Not a Wonderful World: Louis Armstrong Meets Kenny G", in *Jazz Icons: Heroes, Myths and the Jazz Tradition* (Cambridge University Press, 2013), 57–81.

**November 2018: Jazz historiography revisited.** Scott DeVeaux, "Constructing the Jazz Tradition: Jazz Historiography", *Black American Literature Forum* 25, no. 3 (1991): 525–560.

**June 2018: Music cognition.** Vijay Iyer, "Improvisation, Action Understanding, and Music Cognition with and without Bodies" (as above).

**May 2018: Jazz and gender.** Sherrie Tucker, "Big Ears: Listening for Gender in Jazz Studies", *Current Musicology* 71–73 (2002): 375–408.

**April 2018: Music and globalisation.** Ingrid Monson, "Riffs, Repetition, and Theories of Globalization", *Ethnomusicology* 43, no. 1 (1999): 31–65, [doi:10.2307/852693](https://doi.org/10.2307/852693). Martin Stokes, "Music and the Global Order", *Annual Review of Anthropology* 33 (2004): 47–72, [doi:10.1146/annurev.anthro.33.070203.143916](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.anthro.33.070203.143916).

**March 2018: Jazz and Europe.** George McKay, "Introduction", in *Circular Breathing: The Cultural Politics of Jazz in Britain* (Duke University Press, 2005), 1–32. Tony Whyton, "Europe and the New Jazz Studies", in *Eurojazzland*, ed. Luca Cerchiari, Laurent Cugny and Franz Kerschbaumer (Northeastern University Press, 2012), 366–380.

**February 2018: Jazz and race.** Ronald M. Radano and Philip V. Bohlman, eds., "Introduction", *Music and the Racial Imagination* (University of Chicago Press, 2000), 1–53. Martin Stokes, ed., "Introduction", *Ethnicity, Identity and Music: The Musical Construction of Place* (Berg, 1997), 1–27.

**January 2018: Bebop.** Scott DeVeaux, "Eckstine and Herman: A Contrast in Fortunes", in *The Birth of Bebop: A Social and Musical History* (University of California Press, 1997).

**December 2017: Jazz historiography.** Scott DeVeaux, "Constructing the Jazz Tradition" (as above). Gabriel Solis, "Avant-Gardism, the 'Long 1960s' and Jazz Historiography", *Journal of the Royal Musical Association* 131, no. 2 (2006): 331–349, [doi:10.1093/jrma/fkl016](https://doi.org/10.1093/jrma/fkl016).
