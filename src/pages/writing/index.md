<li id="rum">

### [Rum: Negotiating Interaction through Composition](https://www.aicnewmusicjournal.com/articles/rum-negotiating-interaction-through-composition)

*AIC New Music Journal*, 2019. How a composition can work as a focal point for a jazz group's decisions, using Greg Felton's *Rum*. Inspired by Vijay Iyer's rhythmic experiments and Ingrid Monson's writing, it describes our trio's trick of moving between a clave in 7/4 and one in 5/4 that sound almost the same. There's also [a performance of *Rum*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L-WDw2R_JZI).

</li>
