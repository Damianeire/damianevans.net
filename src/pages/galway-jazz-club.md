When I finally cleared the files off an old PC that had been sitting under my desk for years, I was delighted to find records of the club. This is as complete a list as I can put together of everyone who headlined from 2004 until 2007.

Most gigs used a local rhythm section: usually me on bass, Declan O'Donahue or Ken Hall on drums, and John Dunne or the American pianist John King on piano. Danny Healy often sat in on trumpet.

If you played, came along, or have photos, posters or memories from those years, I'd love to hear from you: [damian@damianevans.net](mailto:damian@damianevans.net).

<h2 id="festival">The first Galway Jazz Festival, 2005</h2>

Lorraine and I started the Galway Jazz Festival together with Fidelma Mullane, who was on the board of the Galway Arts Festival. (Lorraine and I got married shortly after the first one.) I was involved until 2008, when I moved to Dublin. Fidelma and others kept it going, it changed hands a few times, and after the 2019 festival the organisers announced they weren't carrying on. I was always delighted to be asked back as a musician. We put a lot of emphasis on education and outreach, and I'm very proud of it.
