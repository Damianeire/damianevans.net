I play double bass, mostly jazz and Irish traditional music, with some orchestral and theatre work along the way. Over the years that has included the Hibernian Orchestra, the Dublin Baroque Players and the RTÉ National Concert Orchestra.

## Selected performances

- An Evening with Brian Byrne and Friends, with Sharon Shannon, Rob Strong, Lucia Evans and others, 2021
- *The Smashing Red Kite's Blues*, children's theatre: The Ark, Dalkey Creates, Meath libraries and Galway Jazz Festival, 2017 to 2019
- Nafplion Music Festival, Greece, with Cormac Kenevey, 2019
- *Angel of Broadway*, RTÉ National Concert Orchestra, National Concert Hall, 2019
- *Warrior*, Karen Egan, Project Arts Centre, 2019
- Tina May and Phil Ware, JJ Smyth's, 2016
- Linley Hamilton Quintet with the Ulster Orchestra, Derry, 2016
- Dana Masters with the BBC Ulster Orchestra, BBC Radio Ulster Gala Concert, 2015
- Stella Bass, *The Music of Sondheim*, Irish tour, 2015
- Emilie Conway Group, Dominican Republic tour, 2015
- Linley Hamilton, British tour, 2015
- Van Morrison, Belfast, 2013
- Cormac Kenevey Quartet: Down with Jazz, UCD Jazz Festival, Howth Jazz Festival, 2013 to 2016

- Cora Smyth, Irish tour and Celtic Connections, Glasgow, 2008
